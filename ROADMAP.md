### Pillar 1: Clinical AI & Interactive Health Calculators (AI Competition Theme)

#### 1. AI Pet Symptom Checker & Health Triage
- **Feature**: An interactive triage wizard where owners describe symptoms (lethargy, vomiting, limping, skin redness) or select body locations on an interactive anatomical pet body map.
- **Output**: Instant triage urgency score (`Routine Check`, `Urgent Care`, `Immediate 24/7 Emergency`), home care first-aid advice, and 1-click routing to book the exact recommended specialist doctor.

#### 2. Smart Breed Nutrition & Daily Caloric Engine
- **Feature**: A clinical calculation tool that factors in species, breed, age, current weight, spayed/neutered status, and activity level.
- **Output**: Generates daily caloric needs ($kcal/day$), exact water intake target ($ml/day$), meal portion recommendations, and links to matching formulated nutrition products in the FurShield store.

---

### Pillar 2: Digital QR Pet Passport & Lost Pet Recovery Card (Real-World Impact)

#### 1. Dynamic Emergency QR Code & Public Lost Pet Profile
- **Feature**: Every pet profile automatically generates an interactive, high-contrast dynamic QR Code.
- **Utility**: In the real world, this QR code can be printed on a pet's collar tag. When scanned by anyone with a smartphone (without logging in):
  - Displays emergency contacts (Owner name, verified phone number, home neighborhood).
  - Shows critical allergies and medical alerts (e.g. *"Diabetic: Requires Insulin"*, *"Severe Penicillin Allergy"*).
  - "Contact Owner" direct button with 1-click call and WhatsApp integration.

#### 2. Professional Printable Medical Passport / Certificate
- **Feature**: A clean, high-resolution printable vaccination and health passport sheet featuring the FurShield verified clinical seal, microchip number, vaccination table, and doctor sign-off.
- **Utility**: Perfect for owners traveling with pets, boarding at kennels, or submitting documentation to airlines.

---

### Pillar 3: Telehealth Virtual Consultation Room (Telemedicine)

#### 1. Integrated WebRTC Virtual Vet Video Consultation
- **Feature**: An interactive video consultation room (`/appointments/{appointment}/room`) built directly into both the Pet Owner and Veterinarian portals.
- **Capabilities**:
  - Live two-way video simulation with microphone toggle, camera toggle, and fullscreen mode.
  - Side-by-side clinical observation notepad for the doctor during the video call.
  - Quick prescription writer directly alongside the live video feed.

---

### Pillar 4: Live Notification Center & Audio Reminders

#### 1. Real-Time Notification Bell with Audio Chimes
- **Feature**: A persistent notification tray across all dashboards with a live badge counter.
- **Triggers**:
  - Upcoming appointment in 24 hours.
  - Vaccination booster due reminder.
  - Adoption application status update.
  - Shelter daily feeding/care confirmation.
- **Sound**: Subtle, pleasant organic audio chime when actions succeed.
